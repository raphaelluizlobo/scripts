# 💫 About Me:
Hi! I’m Raphael Lobo — a bioinformatics / computational biology professional focused on high-performance computing (HPC) and reproducible data workflows. I earned my PhD in Genetics at University of São Paulo and work across genomics, microbiology, and protein structure analysis.<br><br>What I do<br><br>Design and maintain HPC-ready pipelines (batch systems, GPUs, containers)<br><br>Build tools for NGS/omics processing, QC, and downstream analytics<br><br>Run and benchmark protein structure prediction and structure-based discovery workflows<br><br>Turn messy research steps into automated, documented, versioned projects<br><br>Tech I like working with<br><br>Python, Bash, Git, Linux<br><br>Workflow automation (e.g., Snakemake-style orchestration), reproducibility, CI-friendly tooling<br><br>Containers (Singularity/Apptainer, Docker) and GPU compute<br><br>Open-source mindset<br>I value clarity, maintainability, and collaboration: readable code, good docs, and workflows that others can actually run.<br><br>Contact<br>If you want to collaborate, feel free to reach out here on GitHub (or add your preferred contact link).
